Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Every step has the exact text to copy and a picture of what you should see.

Which tools do I need?

Claude (the director) and Higgsfield (pictures and video). ElevenLabs is optional for voices, and CapCut (free) for the final edit.

Can my characters talk?

Yes. The Autopilot Kit shows how to give each character a voice and place the lines on the mouth movement.

Can I create any character I want?

Yes. People, animals, creatures, from a photo or just a description. The guide shows how to turn it into a character and keep it the same in every shot.

Can I post the reels I make?

Yes, the reels you make are yours to post. Check each AI tool's own terms for commercial use.

What's the difference between the two?

The Prompt Pack is for doing it by hand. The Autopilot Kit lets Claude run the whole reel for you, and it includes the Prompt Pack.
